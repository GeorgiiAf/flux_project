🚊 Why Trams Are Detected as Buses
The Reason:
YOLO was trained on the COCO dataset, which includes these vehicle classes:

car
truck
bus
motorcycle
bicycle
But NOT "tram" - because trams aren't common in most countries where COCO was created.

Why This Happens:
Trams and buses are visually very similar:

Both are large, rectangular vehicles
Both have similar dimensions
Both have windows along the sides
Both carry passengers
So YOLO classifies trams as the closest match: bus

Is This a Problem?
No! This is actually fine for your challenge because:

Traffic Analytics Still Work - Object counts, time profiles, and heatmaps are still accurate
It's a Known Limitation - You can mention this in your presentation as a discussion point
Easy to Explain - "The model classifies trams as buses due to visual similarity"
