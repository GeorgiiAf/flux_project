1. Core Goals & Objectives: What We Aim to Achieve
Our primary objective is to develop an AI-powered automated vehicle compliance monitoring system for city authorities.
The project aims to transform raw dashcam footage from public transport and other city-operated vehicles into actionable enforcement data.

Problem We Are Solving
Cities struggle to monitor:
? vehicles with expired technical inspection,
? illegal or blacklisted vehicles,
? cars connected to criminal activity or unpaid penalties,
? and violations occurring across the city in real time.

Current processes are manual, inconsistent, and limited in coverage.

Our Target Users
? City traffic enforcement departments
? Municipal police
? Transport authorities
? National police (for escalated alerts)

Long-Term Vision
To create a scalable, city-wide compliance AI platform that eventually integrates with:
? live CCTV feeds
? public transport fleets
? police patrol cameras
? traffic monitoring infrastructure
and provides a real-time map of vehicle compliance, violations, and risks.

Key Metrics of Success (KPI)
? Detection accuracy of license plates (high-confidence OCR).
? Latency: time from video frame ? alert sent.
? Coverage: number of vehicles/areas monitored.
? Reduction in manual checks needed by police.
? Successful alerts verified by city authorities.

2. Minimum Viable Product (MVP): Our First Step to Success
The MVP focuses on solving the critical pain point:

Automatically identifying non-compliant vehicles from dashcam video and notifying authorities.

We will build a minimal, working pipeline in Google Colab, using: (Python?)
? YOLO for vehicle detection
? License Plate Detection Model
? OCR (Plate Reading)
? A rule-based check against a simple "watchlist" database
The MVP will process a short recorded video, extract plate numbers with high confidence, check them against lists of non-compliant vehicles, and generate a simple violation report.

Core User Problem for MVP
City authorities need a scalable, automated way to find:
? vehicles with expired inspection
? wanted vehicles
? stolen vehicles
   ? blacklisted or illegal vehicles without relying on manual review. The MVP directly addresses this.



3. MVP Key Features
Feature 1 - Vehicle Detection (YOLO Model)
What it does:
Identifies every vehicle in the video frames using YOLO in Google Colab. User benefit:
Provides a reliable visual pipeline to locate vehicles in real-world city footage.


Feature 2 - License Plate Detection + OCR
What it does:
After finding a vehicle, the system detects the license plate region, crops it, and uses OCR to read the plate number.
Includes a confidence threshold to ensure high accuracy and avoid false identifications. User benefit:
Turns visual data into structured, readable information for automated checks.


Feature 3 - Compliance Database Check
What it does:
Compares each detected plate number with a city-maintained list:
? expired technical inspection
? illegal registrations
? stolen/blacklisted vehicles
   ? "red list" targets User benefit:
Automatically identifies vehicles of interest without human intervention.


Feature 4 - Automated Notification Output
What it does:
For each matched violation, the system produces a structured record:
? license plate number
? GPS/time metadata (or frame timestamp)
   ? violation category (reason for the match) This alert can be forwarded to:
? municipal police
? national police
   ? traffic enforcement/ticketing authority User benefit:
Gives enforcement teams actionable, verified intelligence.



"Nice-to-Have" Features (Not in MVP)
? Real-time video processing instead of recorded video
? GPS integration from the bus/tram system
? Integration with national vehicle registries
? Automatic anonymization (face/plate blurring for compliant vehicles)
? A full web dashboard with live monitoring
? Behavior analysis (speeding, illegal parking, lane misuse)
