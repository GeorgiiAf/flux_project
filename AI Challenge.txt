AI Traffic Insights Challenge - Team Brief

Role & Story
You're a startup offering dashcam analytics to city authorities. Your mission: turn raw video into actionable insights for safety and traffic management.
You will receive two short videos of Tallinn city:

* Morning / low-light clip
* Daytime clip

Use AI in any form (LLMs + ML/CV) to analyze them, extract as much useful information as you can, and pitch your service.


Team Setup
* Team name: invent a company name (logo optional).
* Team size: 4-6 students.
* Tools: Your choice. Hints: Google Colab + YOLO (or any detector), optional tracker (ByteTrack/SORT), Python. LLMs are encouraged for coding help, ideas, and reporting.


What to Deliver (presentation + package)
1) 5-7 minute presentation

Explain clearly:

1. What you attempted & why (your product idea, user value for a city).

2. Process & pipeline (thinking steps, models, prompts, metrics).
3. Results & insights (figures/plots/short annotated clips).
4. Novelty (what AI enabled that's new or better than status quo).
5. What was easy vs. challenging with AI/LLMs/ML in practice.
6. One home-country application (where would your approach be useful?).
7. Risks & ethics (privacy, fairness, false positives; mitigation ideas).

2) Hand-in package (zip or repo)

* README.md - how to run your code (Colab link with steps), dependencies, brief summary.
* notebooks/ or src/ - source code used.
* prompts/ - a simple text file of your LLM prompts + a 1-3 sentence note on how you validated outputs.
* figures/ - plots, screenshots, short annotated clips (=20-30 s each).
* slides/ - your presentation (PDF or PPTX).
* (Optional) data/ - small derived CSVs (detections, tracks, metrics).


Minimum Requirements (baseline)
From each video:

* Object detection with counts per class (cars, trucks/buses, pedestrians, cyclists, etc.).
* Time profile (objects per second or per 5 s).
* Spatial pattern (heatmap of detection centers or occupancy by region).
* At least one deeper metric of your choice, e.g.:
o Line crossings ? flow / headways
o Queue length over time in a defined ROI
o Dwell time (stopped vs. moving)
o Pedestrian exposure in carriageway
o Traffic sign crops (stop/traffic light/speed sign)
         o Simple speed estimate (pixel?meter via lane-width assumption) Compare morning vs. daytime (what differs and why it matters for a city).


Stretch Ideas (pick any)
* Tracking with IDs ? trajectories, dwell times, conflict hotspots.
* Rough speed distributions (lane-width calibration).
* TTC proxy (time-to-collision) between vehicles & pedestrians.
* Region-aware insights (lanes, crosswalk, curb/parking) drawn as polygons.
* OCR on sign crops (e.g., read speed limits).
* Anomaly moments (occluded/low-confidence frames) and how you handle them.
* Auto-report draft with an LLM ? then human-edited.


How to Use LLMs (expected)
* Coding helper: request small functions (CSV aggregation, line-cross detection, ROI tests, plotting) and explain the code in comments.
* Design assistant: ask for metric ideas + validation checks.
* Writing aid: draft a short executive summary-then verify and edit.

Include all prompts in your hand-in (keep them concise).
